Which side quests go in the game?

These appear randomly when a team gets punished — completing them reduces or skips the drinks. Select all you want included.

🎬

Dumb Charades

Cross-pair: One player from the punished pair acts out a movie/song/show. Their partner (or another pair) must guess. Get it right → skip drinks. Categories: Bollywood, Hollywood, TV shows, songs.

⚡

Rapid Fire

Cross-pair: The punished pair gets 30 seconds to name 5 things in a category (e.g. "name 5 Marvel heroes", "name 5 cricket players"). Each correct answer = 1 drink saved. Wrong = normal punishment.

🔥

Roast Round

Group vote: The punished player roasts someone in the room. The group votes thumbs up/down. Majority says it's good → drinks halved. This one gets personal fast.

🧠

Trivia Lifeline

Solo: The punished player gets a trivia question (pop culture, sports, movies). Answer correctly → skip punishment. Wrong → drinks doubled. High risk, high reward.

😈

Truth or Dare Swap

Cross-pair: Instead of drinking, the team must complete a dare chosen by another pair. The other pair picks from a list shown on their phone. Can be declined — but then drinks are doubled.

🤝

Alliance Offer

Cross-pair: The punished pair can offer their drinks to another pair as a "bribe". The other pair votes to accept or reject. If accepted, they drink instead — but they earn a Shield token for next round.

🎭

Mimicry Challenge

Cross-pair: Impersonate a celebrity, movie character, or someone in the room for 15 seconds. Group votes. Win = drinks halved. Fail = normal drinks + everyone laughs at you.

🔮

Prediction Gamble

Cross-pair: Before the next card flip, the punished pair makes a prediction about another pair's upcoming guess (right or wrong). Correct prediction = drinks transferred to that pair instead.

Select as many as you want — more options = more chaos
