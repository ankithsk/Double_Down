Toggle Inside Lobby

Teams/Solo toggle sits at the top of the lobby. Switch mid-setup. In Solo mode, pair badges disappear and everyone plays for themselves.

Solo mode gameplay changes

  • No partner response mechanic (no Double Down / Shield buttons)
  • Round 1: everyone guesses simultaneously, but individually scored
  • Rounds 2–4: each person guesses for themselves, drinks are personal
  • Bus ride: highest drink count player rides solo (flipper = decider = same person)
  • Side quests still cross-player — you challenge someone else in the room
